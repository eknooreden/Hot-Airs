
# ☕ HOT AIRS

> ***⚠️ NOTICE:** This game is still in development! The **Upgrades** button currently has only 1 item, and some exciting features haven't been added yet.*
>
> *The game isn't quite an adventure game right now, but it will be once it's complete!*
>
> *Also, if you're playing this on mobile, there will be lots of lag. I'm currently trying to fix that, and audio won't load properly either.*

In this game, you're a... well, a classic, nice, warm cup of hot cocoa. ☕

---

## STORY MODE

Once upon a time, a grandma named **Grandma Cocoa** went on a lovely camping trip deep in the forest.

As she sat beneath the beautiful night sky, gazing at the stars, she enjoyed her favorite drink: a nice, warm cup of hot chocolate.

**That's you, by the way.**

She loved hot chocolate so much that everyone started calling her Grandma Cocoa.

Grandma Cocoa had always loved camping. She went on trips all the time!

But this time...

Something strange happened.

Something you'd never forget.

Something that would stay in your heart forever.

### SHE LEFT YOU.

**JUST BECAUSE YOU GOT COLD.**

Seriously?!

But you couldn't bring yourself to hate her. You two had shared so many wonderful memories, and there were still so many adventures to have and people to meet.

So you decided to set out on a journey to find her.

But then...

You noticed something strange in a nearby bush.

A bright, yet faint glow.

Curious, you headed over (somehow lol) to investigate.

And there, hidden among the leaves, you found...

**WAKJHSD;FLKAJS WHAATTTTT?!**

### WINGS?!?!

At first, you figured they were just part of a Halloween costume. After all, Halloween was coming soon.

So, naturally, you decided to put them on.

Suddenly, your eyes flashed black and white for five seconds.

And then...

**YOU COULD FLY.**

You tried jumping.

But instead of coming back down...

You just kept going.

**UP.**

**AND UP.**

**AND UPPPP.**

W-well... **AMAZING THEN!**

Maybe, just maybe, you could fly high enough to spot Grandma Cocoa's house!

And so, you—a literal mug of hot chocolate—begin your journey.

### Up, up, and away!

Will you ever find Grandma Cocoa?

**Only one way to find out...**

---

## 🏆 SCORE MODE

No complicated story here!

Just keep flying upward and try to get the **highest score possible!**

Think *Flappy Bird*, except you're a flying mug of hot cocoa.

Because why not?

**How high can YOU go?**

---

## 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

### Controls

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **W / ↑** | Fly upward |
| **A / ←** | Move left |
| **D / →** | Move right |
| **Mouse** | Mouse controls supported |

### ☀️ DAYTIME

- Beautiful pumpkin vines grow out from the sides.
- Pumpkins grow along the vines.
- Watch out for **moving vines!**

### 🌙 NIGHTTIME

- Things get **SPOOOOKYYY!**
- Jack-o'-lanterns appear on wooden rods.
- Some obstacles move.
- And some are just there to **TROLL YOU. LOL.**

**Stay alert!** You never know what might pop out next.
